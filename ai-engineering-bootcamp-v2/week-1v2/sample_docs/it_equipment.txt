SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — ExampleCo is a fictional company created for RAG testing. This is not official Northwind or Maven material.

ExampleCo IT Equipment Policy (effective 15 February 2026). Covers issuance, replacement, and purchase of ExampleCo-owned devices for office and approved hybrid staff.

Standard kit. Full-time employees receive one company laptop, one headset, and one security key at onboarding. Office staff may use a shared dock and monitor. Hybrid staff may request one 27-inch ExampleCo-standard monitor for home use after 90 days.

Orders. Employees must not buy hardware on personal cards and expense it. All device orders go through the IT Service Desk catalogue. The IT Operations Manager approves catalogue items up to £400. Items from £400.01 to £1,200 require the Head of IT. Anything above £1,200 also needs Finance.

This is not the Expenses Policy. A line manager’s £250 expense approval does not authorise a laptop or monitor. Remote-work approval also does not authorise hardware spend.

Replacement. Lost, stolen, or damaged devices must be reported to IT within one business day. Theft requires a police report. Replacement before the 36-month refresh cycle is charged to the employee’s cost centre unless accidental-damage cover applies.

Return. Devices are returned to IT at the London or Dublin hub on the last working day. Home monitors ship with the prepaid IT label.
