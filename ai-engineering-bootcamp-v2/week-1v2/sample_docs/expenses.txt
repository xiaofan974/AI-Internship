SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — ExampleCo is a fictional company created for RAG testing. This is not official Northwind or Maven material.

ExampleCo Employee Expenses Policy (effective 1 April 2026). Use this policy for travel, meals, client entertainment, and miscellaneous out-of-pocket costs. Do not use it to buy computers, phones, or monitors. Those follow the IT Equipment Policy, including when the employee works from home.

Submission. Enter claims in the expense tool within 30 days of the transaction. Receipts are required for £10 or more. Missing receipts above £25 are rejected.

Approval. Line managers may approve a single submission up to £250. Claims from £250.01 to £1,000 need director approval. Amounts above £1,000 need Finance. Do not split one purchase to stay under a threshold.

Travel. Standard-class rail is the default. UK flights need director approval. Hotels are capped at £160 per night in London and £120 elsewhere unless a conference rate is pre-approved.

Meals. Overnight travel meals may be claimed up to £35 per person per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable except one drink per person at a pre-approved client dinner.

Home working. Approved hybrid employees may claim broadband of up to £30 per month here. Home furniture is not allowed. Claims described as remote kit, laptop, monitor, or headset will be returned for filing under IT Equipment.

Corporate cards are for business costs only. Personal spend must be repaid within 14 days.
