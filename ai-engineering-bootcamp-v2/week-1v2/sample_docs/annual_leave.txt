SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — ExampleCo is a fictional company created for RAG testing. This is not official Northwind or Maven material.

ExampleCo Annual Leave Policy (effective 1 January 2026). Covers full-time and part-time employees. Contractors are excluded.

Entitlement. Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave plus English public holidays observed by ExampleCo. Part-time leave is pro-rated by contracted hours. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year. Any remainder above 5 days is forfeited on 31 January unless People Operations grants a written exception.

Booking. Requests of 5 days or more must be submitted in the People portal at least 15 working days in advance. Shorter absences need 5 working days’ notice. Managers should respond within 3 working days.

Blackout. Product Engineering has a release blackout in the last two weeks of March and September. Leave in those windows also needs director approval.

Sickness during leave. Days may be converted to sick leave only if the employee provides a fit note and notifies their manager on the first day of illness.

Pay. ExampleCo does not pay unused leave except on termination. Buying extra leave is not offered.

Escalation. Balance disputes go first to the line manager, then to People Operations at people@exampleco.example, which is the final interpreter of this policy.
